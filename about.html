About Us

AARNA USA supplies high-quality OTC medicines and medical supplies to healthcare institutions, pharmacies, and retailers nationwide with precision and dependable service.

What We Do

AARNA USA supplies AARNA-brand OTC medicines and medical supplies, with a primary focus on healthcare institutions. We also serve pharmacies and retailers and offer private-label OTC products for businesses seeking products under their own brand.

PROGRAM PARTICIPATION Federal & State Market Access

Government Programs & Market Access

AARNA USA participates in the Medicaid Drug Rebate Program administered by the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) and the federal 340B Drug Pricing Program. Eligible AARNA pharmaceutical products are also listed in the New York Medicaid (eMedNY) Pharmacy List of Reimbursable Drugs. Product coverage and reimbursement are subject to applicable program requirements.

Medicaid Drug Rebate Program

Participation under federal Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) oversight, supporting nationwide Medicaid coverage.

340B Drug Pricing Program

Compliant pricing and supply options for federal 340B-covered entities, community health clinics, and safety-net providers.

New York Medicaid (eMedNY)

Eligible OTC products listed on the official pharmacy reimbursable drug schedule under AARNA labeler code 82568.
